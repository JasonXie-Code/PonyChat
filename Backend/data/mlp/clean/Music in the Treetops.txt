# Music in the Treetops

对于这首歌部分片段的重奏，参阅Find the Music in You#重奏。

Music in the Treetops

剧集
幕后歌声
角色
小蝶

《Music in the Treetops》是《我的小马驹：友谊就是魔法》第四季的第十二首歌曲，出现于第十四集“幕后歌声”。这首歌最初由小蝶演唱，随后在《Find the Music in You》中同梦之声合唱团一起合唱。这首歌为原声专辑《Songs of Harmony》的第七首歌曲。

这首歌的伴奏版本可以在“My Little Pony: Puzzle Party”中小蝶主题关卡中听到。

歌词

[鸟鸣声]

小蝶: Oh, good morning, little friends. Your singing is oh-so pretty.

[鸟鸣声]

小蝶: Yes, it is a very beautiful day today. Certainly something worth singing about.

There's music in the treetops

And there's music in the vale

And all around the music fills the sky

There's music by the river

And there's music in the grass

And the music makes your heart soar in reply

[vocalizing]
