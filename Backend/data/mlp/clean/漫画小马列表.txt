# 漫画小马列表

This is a sortable list of memorable ponies which appear in the IDW comics or other comics. Official names are in bold. These names are mentioned in the comics, on Hasbro's toy line, or stated to be official by the comic's crew. Names that are not in bold are placeholder names which are unofficial and will soon be up for vote. Names in gray are up for vote right now. Colors are approximate.
