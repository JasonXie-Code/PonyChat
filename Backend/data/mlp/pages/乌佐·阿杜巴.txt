乌佐·阿杜巴是一位美国演员、歌手，在《我的小马驹大电影》中为诺沃女王配音。

2016年4月，《The Hollywood Reporter》首次宣布了阿杜巴参与这部电影的消息，[1]阿杜巴随后于2017年2月在Twitter上证实了这一点。[2]

Outside My Little Pony, Aduba is best known for her Emmy Award-winning portrayal of prisoner Suzanne "Crazy Eyes" Warren in the Netflix original series Orange Is the New Black. Her first television appearance was as a nurse on Blue Bloods in 2012. She also appeared on the NBC live presentation of The Wiz Live! as Glinda, and she was a guest star on Saturday Night Live in 2014. Most recently, Aduba provided the voice of Bismuth in Steven Universe.

In theater, Aduba first gained acting recognition in 2003 for her performance in Translations of Xhosa at the Olney Theatre Center for the Arts, which earned her a Helen Hayes Award nomination for "Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Play". She has also played Amphiarus in New York Theatre Workshop and La Jolla Playhouse productions of The Seven, Toby in Helen Edmundson's adaptation of Coram Boy, and the title character of Venice at the New York Public Theater. Aduba also sang "By My Side" as part of the original revival cast of Godspell at the Circle in the Square Theatre.

In film, Aduba appeared in the Netflix original film Tallulah and the 2016 film adaptation of American Pastoral.
