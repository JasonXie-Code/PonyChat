夏洛特·富勒顿（英语：Charlotte Fullerton）是《我的小马驹：友谊就是魔法》的一位动画编剧和作者。

富勒顿最初由她的朋友编剧艾米·基廷·罗杰斯向劳伦·浮士德引荐，从而加入了这部动画的制作团队。她有时会与编剧兼电影制作人的凯文·鲁维奥进行合作，曾参与过《决斗大师》的制作，并且同他合作为《Find A Pet Song》作词。[1]

第39届日间艾美奖上，借由她对本剧的贡献，富勒顿凭借《Find A Pet Song》这首歌曲而获得了中“最具创意的儿童动画音乐奖”的提名。[2]

Fullerton's professional credits include writing for Power Rangers, the second season of Digimon: Digital Monsters, Duel Masters, Zatch Bell!, Kim Possible, Ben 10: Alien Force/Ultimate Alien/Omniverse, The Super Hero Squad Show, The Fairly OddParents, Generator Rex, Green Lantern: The Animated Series, and Avengers Assemble. She was also a story editor for Duel Masters, Omniverse, and Zevo-3.

Fullerton served as voice director, post-production supervisor, and foley artist on Rubio's parody short film Troops, and she has written book adaptations of Ben 10: Alien Force and Sonic X. She is the widow of animation and comic book writer Dwayne McDuffie.
